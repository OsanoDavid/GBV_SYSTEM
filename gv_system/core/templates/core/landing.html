About Us

The Digital Safe Space platform is an initiative of the State Department for Gender and Affirmative Action, a government department responsible for coordinating national efforts to promote gender equality, protect vulnerable populations, and eliminate gender-based violence in Kenya. The department operates under the Ministry of Gender, Culture, the Arts and Heritage and plays a central role in developing policies, programmes, and partnerships that advance gender equality and social inclusion across the country. Through its work with national and county governments, civil society organizations, and development partners, the department supports initiatives aimed at strengthening survivor protection systems, promoting women's empowerment, and addressing gender-based violence both offline and in digital spaces.

Recognizing Online Violence

Understanding Technology-Facilitated Gender-Based Violence

TFGBV involves the use of digital tools—such as social media, messaging apps, and tracking devices—to harass, stalk, or abuse individuals. Recognizing these patterns is the first step toward safety and digital bodily autonomy. Click a category below to view details.

Cyberstalking involves persistent, unwanted online observation or messaging aimed at intimidating or controlling a victim.
Doxing is the act of publishing private information about an individual (like address or phone number) without consent.
Impersonation occurs when someone creates a fraudulent profile using your name or photos to harm your reputation or commit fraud.
Hate speech and cyber mobs include targeted insults, coordinated raids, or threats made against individuals based on their identity or gender online.
